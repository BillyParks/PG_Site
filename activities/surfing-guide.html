A Beginner's Guide to Surfing in Burleigh Heads (2025)

You can't come to Burleigh Heads and not feel the pull of the ocean. The sight of surfers effortlessly gliding along the world-famous point break is mesmerizing. But what if you've never stepped on a board before? Good news! While Burleigh is home to a pro-level wave, it's also one of the best places on the Gold Coast for beginners to get started. This guide covers everything you need to know about **surfing in Burleigh Heads**, from the safest spots to the best local lessons.

Where to Surf: A Tale of Two Beaches

The first thing to understand is that Burleigh has different surf zones for different skill levels. Knowing where to go is key to having a fun and safe experience.

For Experts: The Point Break

The wave you see wrapping perfectly around the headland from the lookout? That's "The Point." It's a world-class wave for experienced surfers only. The paddle-out is challenging, and the wave is fast and powerful. It's fantastic to watch, but it is not the place to learn.

For Beginners: The Main Beach & "The Cove"

The main stretch of Burleigh beach, away from the point, offers much gentler, rolling whitewater waves that are absolutely perfect for beginners. Further north, near the headland but protected from the main swell, is an area often called "The Cove" which can be an ideal, sheltered spot on smaller days. This is where most **Burleigh Heads surf lessons** take place.

Burleigh Heads Surf Lessons

Taking a lesson is by far the best way to start your surfing journey. You'll learn about ocean safety, board control, and the basics of popping up, all under the watchful eye of a qualified instructor. Here are some highly-rated local options.

  • Godfathers of the Ocean Surf School: Run by local legend Michael "Munga" Barry, this is one of the most authentic and respected surf schools in the area. They specialize in group and private lessons for all ages and are known for their focus on safety and fun.
  • Get Wet Surf School: While their main base is a short drive away, they frequently run lessons at Burleigh and other suitable beginner locations on the Gold Coast. They are a very professional and popular operation.

Surfboard Hire in Burleigh

If you've had a lesson or two and just want to practice, hiring a board is a great option. Look for a soft-top "foamie" board, as they are stable and safer for beginners.

  • Hot Stuff Surfboards: An iconic, old-school surf shop right in the heart of Burleigh that offers board rentals. You can find them on Connor Street.
  • Hire-A-Board Gold Coast: A convenient local service that can often deliver boards directly to your accommodation.

Beginner Surf Safety & Etiquette

  • Swim Between the Flags: Before you even think about surfing, understand where it's safe to swim. The flagged areas are for swimmers, not surfers.
  • Never Surf Alone: Always go out with a friend.
  • Stay in the Whitewater: As a beginner, stay in the broken, white waves closer to shore. Don't paddle out "the back" where experienced surfers are.
  • Control Your Board: The #1 rule. Always hold onto your board. A loose board can be dangerous for you and others.
  • Look Both Ways: Be aware of your surroundings, especially other surfers and swimmers.
