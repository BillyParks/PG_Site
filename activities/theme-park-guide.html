The Ultimate Guide to Gold Coast Theme Parks (From Burleigh Heads)

The Gold Coast is rightfully known as Australia's theme park capital, and for many, a holiday isn't complete without a day of thrilling rides or amazing animal encounters. The great news is that staying in the relaxed, beachside suburb of Burleigh Heads gives you the best of both worlds: a peaceful coastal base that's just a short drive from all the action. This guide will break down the "Big 5" parks and give you the essential info for planning your day trip.

Warner Bros. Movie World

Step into Hollywood on the Gold Coast. Movie World is all about blockbuster movies, DC superheroes, and thrilling rollercoasters. You can meet characters like Batman and Bugs Bunny, watch the incredible Main Street parade, and test your courage on some of Australia's best rides.

  • Best For: Thrill-seekers, movie buffs, and families with kids of all ages.
  • Don't Miss: The DC Rivals HyperCoaster (for adrenaline junkies) and the Kids' WB! Fun Zone for little ones.

Sea World

More than just a theme park, Sea World is a marine conservation park with incredible animal exhibits, live shows, and a handful of family-friendly rides. It's the best place to see dolphins, seals, penguins, and even polar bears up close.

  • Best For: Animal lovers and families with younger children.
  • Don't Miss: The Affinity Dolphin Presentation is a must-see. The new Atlantis precinct also offers some more thrilling rides.

Wet'n'Wild

On a hot Queensland day, there's no better place to be. Wet'n'Wild is a massive water park packed with giant slides, a lazy river, and a huge wave pool. It's pure, splash-filled fun.
