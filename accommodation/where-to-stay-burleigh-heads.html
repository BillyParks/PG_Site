Where to Stay in Burleigh Heads: Accommodation Guide

Burleigh Heads is celebrated for its relaxed coastal vibe, golden beaches, and thriving food scene. Whether you’re planning a family holiday, a romantic escape, or a solo adventure, this guide spotlights the best accommodation options in Burleigh Heads and nearby, blending local insights and authentic guest feedback.

Top Beachfront Apartments

Burleigh Beach Tower

Burleigh Beach Tower stands directly opposite the sand, offering self-contained one- and two-bedroom apartments with panoramic ocean views. Guests consistently praise the modern, spotless rooms and attentive staff. The property features a swimming pool, tennis court, and easy access to Burleigh’s shops and restaurants, making it a favourite for couples and families.

Bujerum Apartments on Burleigh

This boutique property offers just five exclusive apartments, each with spacious living areas, full kitchens, and private balconies overlooking the surf. The minimalist décor and tranquil setting create a true coastal retreat. Its enviable location puts you steps from the beach and a short stroll to James Street’s bustling cafes and boutiques.

Burleigh Esplanade Apartments

Ideal for families and groups, Burleigh Esplanade Apartments provide open-plan layouts, separate bedrooms, and generous balconies with sweeping views. Facilities include a heated pool, spa, gym, and tennis court. Many guests highlight the easy walk to both the beach and Burleigh Head National Park as a major advantage.

Family-Friendly Resorts

Paradise Grove Holiday Apartments

Set among six acres of lush tropical gardens, Paradise Grove Holiday Apartments is a centrally located gem in Burleigh Heads. The property offers spacious, self-contained two- and three-bedroom apartments, making it a popular choice for families and groups. Onsite amenities include two swimming pools, a tennis court, barbecue areas, and secure parking. Guests appreciate the peaceful setting, friendly management, and the convenience of being just a short walk from Burleigh Beach, James Street’s dining precinct, and the national park. The relaxed atmosphere and practical facilities make Paradise Grove a standout option for those seeking comfort and value in the heart of Burleigh.

Swell Resort Burleigh Beach

A favourite among families, Swell Resort offers one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments with full kitchens and laundry facilities. The resort’s amenities include a heated magnesium pool, spa, tennis court, gym, and barbecue area. Its location puts you close to both the beach and Burleigh’s vibrant dining scene, making it easy to enjoy everything the area has to offer.

Gemini Court Holiday Apartments

Perched on Burleigh Headland, Gemini Court is known for its breathtaking ocean views and direct access to the national park. Spacious, well-equipped apartments suit families and groups, while the pool, tennis court, and free parking add convenience. Guests frequently mention the excellent location for both beach days and scenic walks.

Boutique and Budget Options

The Seaport Burleigh Airbnb

For travellers seeking a personalised stay, The Seaport Burleigh offers stylish interiors, luxurious bedding, and thoughtful touches throughout. This Airbnb is ideal for couples or small families who want a home-away-from-home experience in the heart of Burleigh.

Burleigh Gold Coast Motel

Located at Burleigh’s northern end, this motel provides modern, affordable rooms with easy access to both Burleigh and Miami’s lively neighbourhood. Amenities include free Wi-Fi, a swimming pool, and laundry facilities, making it a practical choice for budget-conscious travellers.

Upmarket and Luxury Stays

Sandbar Burleigh

Sandbar Burleigh delivers elegant, coastal-inspired apartments just a short stroll from Burleigh’s main attractions. Each unit features a modern kitchen, spacious living area, and private balcony. Onsite amenities include a lap pool, fitness centre, and yoga deck. Ocean-view apartments are especially popular and tend to book up early.

Boardwalk Burleigh Beach

These contemporary apartments are ideal for families or groups seeking a modern, beachside escape. Facilities include a heated pool, barbecue area, and yoga terrace, with the beach just across the road. Secure underground parking is a welcome bonus in this busy area.

Insider Tips for Booking

  • Book early for peak holiday periods, especially for ocean-view apartments and boutique stays.
  • Many properties offer discounts for longer stays or mid-week bookings.
  • Check each property’s official website or trusted booking platforms for the latest availability, amenities, and special packages.
  • For families, look for accommodation with full kitchens, laundry facilities, and proximity to parks or playgrounds.
  • Couples may prefer boutique apartments or luxury resorts with spa and wellness facilities.

Slightly Beyond Burleigh: Notable Nearby Options

If you’re open to staying just outside Burleigh Heads, consider Crystal Pacific, which is a five-minute drive away and known for its stylish décor and tranquil setting. Miami, just north of Burleigh, offers a lively atmosphere and easy access to both Burleigh and Broadbeach, with a range of accommodation from boutique motels to luxury apartments.

Why Stay in Burleigh Heads?

Choosing accommodation in Burleigh Heads places you at the heart of the Gold Coast’s most beloved beach community. You’ll enjoy a blend of natural beauty, friendly hospitality, and a wealth of amenities. With options ranging from family-friendly resorts like Paradise Grove to luxury beachfront apartments and cozy boutique stays, there’s a perfect fit for every traveller.

Whether you’re seeking a relaxing beachfront escape, a family-friendly base, or a stylish boutique retreat, Burleigh Heads offers a diverse range of accommodation to suit every taste and budget. The unbeatable location, welcoming atmosphere, and proximity to beaches, parks, and local dining ensure your Gold Coast adventure starts with the perfect place to stay.
