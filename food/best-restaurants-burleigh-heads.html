Best Restaurants in Burleigh Heads: Local’s Guide

Burleigh Heads has earned its reputation as one of the Gold Coast’s most vibrant dining suburbs. From beachfront fine dining to tucked-away neighbourhood gems, this local’s guide highlights the must-visit restaurants—both within Burleigh Heads and just beyond—to satisfy every craving. Authentic visitor reviews from TripAdvisor and local insights ensure you’ll discover the best of Burleigh’s culinary scene in 2025.

1. Rick Shores

Situated right on Burleigh Beach, Rick Shores combines panoramic ocean views with modern Asian-fusion cuisine. Its light-filled dining room and al fresco deck are ideal for sunset meals. Signature shared plates like the Moreton Bay bug roll and pork xiao long bao earn rave reviews for bold flavours. Reservations open two months in advance—request a window table for the best perspective.

2. Restaurant Labart

Tucked on West Street, Restaurant Labart is Burleigh’s premier fine-dining destination. The seasonally driven European-bistro menu features dishes such as spanner crab risotto and roasted spatchcock, plated with artistry and paired with a well-curated wine selection. Reviewers commend the polished service and intimate dining room, which evokes the charm of a Parisian backstreet.

3. Burleigh Pavilion & The Tropic

Overlooking Burleigh Beach, these sister venues share prime real estate and complementary menus. Burleigh Pavilion offers a laid-back pub atmosphere with wood-fired pizzas and fresh salads, while The Tropic leans into Mediterranean-inspired seafood and share plates. Both spots draw friendly locals for casual meals and ocean breezes.

4. Rabbath Lebanese Bistro

Rabbath has built a loyal following for authentic Lebanese fare. Hummus, falafel and grilled skewers arrive with freshly baked flatbreads, and generous mezze spreads are perfect for groups. Visitors praise the warm hospitality and consistent quality that blends tradition with contemporary touches.

5. Willow Dining

This intimate Mediterranean-Spanish restaurant on James Street earns praise for its tapas-style sharing plates. Dishes such as patatas bravas and char-grilled octopus reflect careful ingredient sourcing and creative flavour profiles. Couples and small groups appreciate Willow’s cosy atmosphere and thoughtful wine pairings.

6. The Little Plate

Located just off the main strip, The Little Plate serves modern Australian cuisine in a warm, wood-lined dining room. The slow-cooked lamb shoulder with caramelized shallots and inventive daily specials stand out. Its relaxed yet refined vibe attracts locals seeking a quieter dinner option.

7. Jimmy Wah’s

Jimmy Wah’s brings fragrant Vietnamese flavours with a modern Australian twist. Window seats offer people-watching on Gold Coast Highway, while dishes like soft-shell crab banh mi and pork belly with watermelon radish highlight the chef’s balance of texture and seasoning. The restaurant’s approachable yet polished service garners frequent praise.

8. Commune & Paddock Bakery

For breakfast and brunch, locals flock to Commune and Paddock Bakery. Commune’s avocado smash and specialty coffee create a laid–back vibe, while Paddock Bakery’s house-made pastries and sourdough loaves often draw queues at opening time. Both offer vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options.

9. Quest Burleigh Café & Social Brew

Nestled on James Street, Quest Burleigh Café serves artisan coffee and hearty toasties with vegan milk alternatives in an intimate 12-seat space. Social Brew—inside Burleigh Arcade—features plant-filled décor, speedy service and standout breakfast dishes like avocado on toast. Visitors praise their friendly baristas and consistent quality.

10. Maman Bar & Kitchen

Just north of Burleigh Heads, Maman offers a Mediterranean-inspired menu in a bright, modern space. Plates such as za’atar chicken and moussaka benefit from generous portion sizes and bold spices. Reviewers note the relaxed atmosphere, making it a great spot for both family dinners and friendly catch-ups.

11. Hidden Gems and Slightly Beyond

  • Labart’s tasting menus in West Burleigh for elevated multi-course experiences.
  • MC Sandwich House on West Burleigh Road for generously loaded gourmet sandwiches and inventive vegetarian options.
  • Oi Izakaya on Gold Coast Highway for authentic Japanese izakaya dining, including sashimi and small plates in a convivial setting.

Insider Tips for Dining in Burleigh Heads

  • Arrive early or book ahead for beachfront and James Street restaurants, especially on weekends.
  • Weekday lunch visits often have lighter crowds and shorter wait times.
  • Public transport via the G:link and local bus routes stops near James Street; free parking is available along James Street before 9 AM.
  • Check each restaurant’s website or social media for up-to-date menus, specials and reservation links.

With so many outstanding options, Burleigh Heads caters to every taste—from casual café brunches to special-occasion dinners. By following this local’s guide, you’ll experience the best culinary offerings of this dynamic coastal suburb.
