Best Restaurants in Burleigh Heads: Local’s Guide

Burleigh Heads is one of the Gold Coast’s most vibrant dining suburbs. This local’s guide rounds up the must-visit restaurants—from beachfront icons to tucked-away neighbourhood gems—so you can taste the very best of Burleigh in 2025.

1. Rick Shores

Panoramic ocean views meet modern Asian-fusion cuisine. Reserve a window table and don’t miss the signature Moreton Bay bug roll.

2. Restaurant Labart

Burleigh’s premier fine-dining spot serves a seasonal European-bistro menu with polished service and a curated wine list.

3. Burleigh Pavilion & The Tropic

Sister venues overlooking the beach. The Pavilion offers laid-back pub fare while The Tropic focuses on Mediterranean-inspired seafood.

4. Rabbath Lebanese Bistro

Authentic Lebanese cuisine with warm hospitality. Mezze plates, hummus and grilled skewers keep locals coming back.

5. Willow Dining

A cosy James Street favourite serving Mediterranean-Spanish tapas-style dishes alongside thoughtful wine pairings.

6. The Little Plate

Modern Australian cuisine in a relaxed, wood-lined dining room. Daily specials and slow-cooked lamb shoulder are standouts.

7. Jimmy Wah’s

Vietnamese flavours meet modern Australian style. Try the pork belly with watermelon radish and watch the world go by from the window seats.

8. Commune & Paddock Bakery

For breakfast and brunch, head to Commune for specialty coffee or Paddock Bakery for house-made pastries and sourdough.

9. Quest Burleigh Café & Social Brew

Both cafés serve excellent coffee and quick bites. Social Brew’s plant-filled décor makes it a relaxing spot to start the day.

10. Maman Bar & Kitchen

Just north of Burleigh, Maman dishes up Mediterranean-inspired plates in a bright, modern space perfect for families and friends.

11. Hidden Gems Nearby

  • Labart’s tasting menus in West Burleigh for elevated multi-course dining.
  • MC Sandwich House on West Burleigh Road for generously loaded gourmet sandwiches.
  • Oi Izakaya on Gold Coast Highway for authentic Japanese small plates.

Insider Tips for Dining in Burleigh Heads

  • Book ahead for beachfront and James Street restaurants, especially on weekends.
  • Weekday lunches often mean shorter wait times.
  • Public transport and early morning parking make getting around easier.
