About Paradise Grove

Welcome to Paradise Grove

Your independent guide to the best of Burleigh Heads and the Gold Coast. Our mission is to help visitors and locals discover the region’s most memorable experiences, from hidden beaches and scenic walks to the top places to eat, stay, and explore.

Our Story

Have you ever spent hours trying to plan a trip, only to end up with a dozen open tabs and more confusion than when you started? We have.

Paradise Grove was born out of that frustration. We love to travel, but we were tired of sifting through endless, unhelpful articles and biased reviews. We wanted a resource we could trust. It had to be clean, easy to navigate, and focused on giving genuinely good advice. So, we decided to build it ourselves.

Our goal is to do the hard work for you. We research the best spots, find the most practical information, and present it in a simple, straightforward guide. We believe that finding what you need should be the easiest part of your trip.

To support our work, we use affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you book through our site (at no additional cost to you). This allows us to keep the site running and continue creating helpful content.

We're just getting started, and we're so glad you're here.
